Who are we?

We, the founding members of the AMIMUN Newsletter team, have created this space to foster literary expression and high standards of ethical and unbiased journalism
The inception of the same began with the motto and determination to transform thoughts into eloquent expressions and uphold the highest editorial integrity within our parent institution–Amity University Uttar Pradesh
and leadership among our members. We are committed to creating a space where aspiring writers and journalists can engage in publishing their work and of course, report with credibility. Through this initiative, we hope to sculpt a legacy of those who not only become pioneers in writing but also leaders in diplomacy.
